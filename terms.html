Terms of use

SWGPush Backups is a private tool for the operator of SWGPush. It is not a public backup-storage service.

Authorized use

The tool is intended for the operator’s own server data and an account they are authorized to use. The operator is responsible for the server and Drive permissions, backup configuration, storage capacity and handling of stored records.

Availability and recovery

The tool and these information pages are provided as available, without a service-level commitment. Successful archive checks do not guarantee recovery in every circumstance. The operator should retain appropriate independent copies and evaluate recovery procedures.

Google Drive access depends on the operator’s authorization, storage availability and Google’s service limits and terms. Access can be revoked, and this integration may be changed or discontinued.

Privacy and contact

Data practices are described in the privacy notice. General project questions can be raised in the information-site repository. Use the support email on the Google consent screen for private data requests.
